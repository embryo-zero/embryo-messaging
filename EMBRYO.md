# Embryo Messaging

Embryo Messaging is Embryo-Zero's fork of [nats-server](https://github.com/nats-io/nats-server) (08f7bd2, commit 08f7bd2). It does lightweight messaging.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
